## Recurring failure patterns worth codifying here

Based on the [Changelog](/changelog) and [Fix Plan](/plan), these are the mistakes that have
actually caused bugs in this codebase — good candidates for a real pre-edit checklist:

- **`penalty_winner` casing** — always compare with `.toLowerCase()`; several bugs came from
  case-sensitive comparisons in `app.js`, `profile.js`, or `live-results.js`.
- **`matchId` vs. `apiFixtureId`** — never assume the live-API `id` equals the internal `matchId`
  for knockout games; join through `fixtures.apiFixtureId`.
- **Source order matters** — Supabase, Worker, and Firestore reads have all drifted out of sync at
  different points (see the 2026-06-22 entries in the changelog); pick one canonical read order per
  view and keep it consistent between the main app and the profile page.
- **`node --check` before shipping** — most worklog entries that touch `app.js` verify with
  `node --check scripts/app.js` (or `docs/scripts/app.js`) before calling the change done.
- **Don't reintroduce the 3-point scoring branch** — it was deliberately removed; see
  [Scoring](/scoring).
