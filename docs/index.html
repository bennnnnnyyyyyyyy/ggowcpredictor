🥅 Penalty Shootout Rules

If a knockout match is drawn after 90 minutes and goes to penalties, your prediction is scored differently from a normal result:

If you predicted a draw+penalty shootout but the game was actually decided outright in normal or extra time (no shootout needed), you still get Correct Result points if the penalty winner you picked matches the team that won the game.

Full points: You predicted a draw and picked the correct penalty shootout winner → full Exact Score points for that stage (e.g. 30 pts at ×2, 45 pts at ×3, etc).
Partial points: You predicted a non-draw scoreline and the team you picked to win went on to win the penalty shootout → Correct Result points for that stage (e.g. 10 pts at ×2, 15 pts at ×3, etc).
Partial points (reverse case): You predicted a draw and picked a penalty winner, but the match was actually settled outright in normal/extra time — if your pen pick matches the team that won, you still get Correct Result points for that stage.
0 pts: You predicted a draw but picked the wrong penalty winner (whether the game went to a shootout or was settled outright), or you predicted a non-draw scoreline and picked the team that lost the shootout.

Note: once a match is decided on penalties, your predicted scoreline's exact goal difference no longer matters — only whether you called the draw/winner and the shootout winner correctly.
